Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Audio stays on your device

Music Mute processes the audio of the tab you turn it on for, entirely inside your Chrome browser, using an on-device AI voice filter. Audio is never recorded, stored, or sent to us or anyone else.

What we collect

  • Email address — only if you choose to sign in (to activate or check a purchased license). We send you a one-time sign-in code to that address.
  • License status — whether your email has a lifetime license, and its source (manual transfer or card payment).
  • Payment details you submit (Egypt, manual payments only) — the payment method, the transaction reference or number you paid from, and an optional receipt screenshot, so we can confirm the transfer.
  • Card payments are handled by our payment provider, Creem (creem.io). We never see your card number; we receive your email and an order ID.

The extension also stores, on your device only, your settings (filter strength, volume), the start date of your free trial, and your sign-in session.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t collect browsing history, page content, or the audio you listen to.
  • We don’t sell or share your data, and we don’t use it for advertising.

Where data is stored

Account and license data are stored with Supabase (our database provider). Receipt screenshots are stored privately and are visible only to you and the Music Mute admin.

Your choices

You can use the free trial without signing in. You can sign out of the extension at any time. To delete your account, license record, or payment submissions, email us at the support address on the pricing page and we’ll delete them within 30 days (we may keep minimal payment records where the law requires it).

Contact

Questions about privacy: use the support email shown on the pricing page.
